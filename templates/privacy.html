Introduction

HabitCheck AI is designed to help you understand and monitor your habits through a privacy-conscious experience.

We collect only the information needed to sign you in, operate the service, and present your dashboard and analytics.

info Information

We aim to keep this policy clear and practical. If you have any questions about how your data is handled, contact us using the details in the Contact Us section.

Information We Collect

We collect information from the account you use, the authentication flow, and your activity within the platform.

Personal information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Google profile photo

Authentication data

  • Firebase UID
  • Login provider
  • Session information

Usage data

  • Habit inputs you choose to save
  • Prediction requests
  • App interactions needed to render results

Device information

  • App version
  • Device model
  • Operating system

Summary

  • We only collect essential account and usage information.
  • We do not collect more than is needed for the product to function.
  • Most usage data is tied to your authenticated account session.
SECTION 03

How We Use Information

We use your information to deliver core app features, keep your account synchronized, and provide support when needed.

Purpose of collection

  • Authenticate your account
  • Synchronize habit data
  • Provide predictions and analytics
  • Improve app performance

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use your data for unrelated advertising.
  • We do not access your Google password.
bolt Use

We use your information only for the product experience you expect: sign-in, syncing, and habit analysis.

  • Your data powers account access and analytics.

  • We do not use your information outside the service.

Firebase Authentication

We use Firebase Authentication to manage login sessions and protect your account.

What it protects

When you choose to sync data, it may be stored in Firebase services used by HabitCheck AI.

Stored data

Retention
