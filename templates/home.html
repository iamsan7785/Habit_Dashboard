HabitCheck AI
Sleep, Stress & Study Monitoring

HabitCheck AI helps you track wellness habits before you sign in

HabitCheck AI is a public wellness app for sleep, stress, and study habit monitoring. It collects lifestyle data — step count, screen time, sleep duration, notifications, and more — from your Android device, then uses machine learning to predict stress, sleep quality, study productivity, and overall health trends.

This is a public homepage. You can review what HabitCheck AI does before logging in: it helps you monitor sleep, stress, and study habits in one place.
